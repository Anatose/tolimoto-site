App

Tolimoto LogBETA

A service and fuel log for your motorcycle and car. It remembers when the oil is due, works out consumption and spending — and keeps everything with you.

  • Fill-up in one tapThe main button on the first screen; swipe to pick the vehicle.
  • Schedule by distance and timeWhat is due and when, with a clear status.
  • Consumption and spendingPer fill-up, per year, and the cost of a kilometre.
  • No sign-up, works offlineYour records live on your phone.
  • Google Sheets — if you wantTwo-way sync; receipt photos on your own Drive.
  • Import from Fuelly and FuelioBring your fill-up history over in a minute.

Tolimoto runs no servers holding your data. Records stay on your device and, if you connect Google, in your own spreadsheet and Drive folder. The app can only see files it created itself. Privacy policy
