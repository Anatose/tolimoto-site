First steps

  1. Add a vehicle. Name, motorcycle or car, currency and the current odometer reading. Leave “Typical set” on and the app fills in a maintenance schedule (oil, filters, chain, brake fluid…) that you can change later.
  2. Log a fill-up. The big blue button on the first screen. Enter the odometer, litres and the receipt total (or the price per litre — the other one is calculated). Tick “Full tank” when you fill to the top: consumption is counted between full tanks.
  3. Several vehicles? Swipe the card at the top of the Garage screen. The fill-up button always works for the vehicle shown.
  4. Odometer only. “Update” on the vehicle card records the mileage without a fill-up — handy before a service.

Service and the schedule

Every service job is a record: date, odometer, category (Service, Consumables, Repair, Accessories), what was done, parts and labour costs, notes and photos of receipts.

  • Items and tags. When you change the oil, mark the item “Engine oil” (tag #engineoil). That is how the app knows when it was done last.
  • Schedule. Each item has an interval in kilometres and/or days. The app shows what is left and colours it: OK, Soon, Overdue.
  • Your own items. “+ Item” on the Schedule screen: a name, a tag, an interval and a note from your owner’s manual (oil volume, torque settings).

Log and statistics

  • Log — all records in one list: search by text and part numbers, filters for service, fill-ups and expenses, date range. Each fill-up shows its consumption: green when it is noticeably below your average, red when well above.
  • Statistics — spending per year (service, accessories, fuel, insurance and other), cost per kilometre, average consumption and charts. Tap a line to see what it is made of.
  • Expenses — insurance, inspection, tax and anything else, via the “+” button.
  • Spending for a period. Set the “From” and “To” dates in the log — a total for that period appears above the list, split into service and repairs, accessories, fuel and other.
  • On a computer the log is a table with columns: date, odometer, type, record, consumption and amount. Click the “Date”, “Odometer” or “Amount” heading to sort.

Full cost of ownership

The usual “Cost per km” in Stats adds up everything you have recorded. The full cost also counts the vehicle losing value — so you see what a kilometre and a month of ownership really cost. It is optional: nothing changes until you turn it on.

  1. Stats → “+ Include the purchase” under the cost per km. Or: Garage → tap the vehicle name → “Full cost”.
  2. Purchase: date, odometer and price.
  3. What it is worth now, roughly. Check prices of similar listings. Once a year the app suggests updating the estimate — it never makes up or “depreciates” values by itself.
  • The tile gets a second figure — “… with purchase”. Tap it for the cost per kilometre and per month, split into parts: loss of value, fuel, service and repair, insurance with inspection and tax, accessories, other.
  • A month of ownership splits into a fixed part (loss of value, insurance, inspection, tax — paid even while the vehicle stands) and a part by distance. Ride less and each kilometre costs more.
  • Sold it? Choose “Sold” and enter the date and price — the calculation stops at the sale date. The app then offers to move the vehicle to the archive, delete it or keep it in the garage.

Selling: the history for the buyer

Garage → tap the vehicle name → “Export history (PDF)”. You get a neat A4 document: all the work with dates and odometer, when the oil, chain and brake fluid were last changed, and receipt photos.

  • What to include. Receipt photos are on. Amounts, fill-ups and notes are off: buyers usually care what and when, not how much, and notes can be personal.
  • Photos come from your Google Drive, so you need to be signed in to Google and online. Without that the table just says “photo”.
  • On iPhone “Create PDF” makes the file right away and opens the Share menu: “Save to Files”, or send it to the buyer by messenger or email. If the menu does not open, tap “Save or send the PDF”.
  • On a computer the PDF file goes to your downloads folder. The PDF is made offline too — only the photos are left out.

Archive, comparison and deleting

  • Archive. Sold it or no longer ride it — Garage → vehicle name → “Move to archive”. It leaves the first screen, the schedule and the reminders, but the whole history stays. “Archive (N)” on the Garage screen or in Settings → “Open”: log, stats, full cost and PDF export. Changed your mind — “Back to the garage”.
  • Compare. In the archive or in Settings pick 2–3 vehicles — current and archived together — and see them side by side: time owned, distance, cost per kilometre (plain and with purchase), per month and consumption.
  • Delete completely — only on purpose. All records of the vehicle are deleted on this device and in the Google Sheet; photos on Google Drive stay. Before deleting, the app offers “Save a JSON copy” and asks you to type the vehicle name.

Moving from Fuelly or Fuelio

  • Fuelly: on fuelly.com open your vehicle → export fill-ups as CSV.
  • Fuelio: make a CSV backup in Fuelio; it keeps one file per vehicle (vehicle-…-sync.csv). You can pick several files at once. Fuelio costs come along too: service jobs go to the log, insurance and taxes to expenses.
  • In Tolimoto Log: Settings → “Import from Fuelly or Fuelio (CSV)”, then choose which vehicle each file goes to. Miles and gallons are converted. Importing the same file again replaces records instead of duplicating them.

Google Sheets and photos

  • Settings → “Sign in with Google”. The app creates a “Tolimoto Log” folder in your Google Drive with a spreadsheet and a “Receipts” folder for photos.
  • Sync runs by itself: when you open the app, after each change, and when the network comes back. You can also edit records in the spreadsheet — the changes come back to the app.
  • The “Summary” tab is built by the app (schedule, current odometer, spending by year). Edit the data on the other tabs.
  • The app can only see the files it created. More in the privacy policy.
  • Phone and computer. Sign in to the same Google account on both — your records are shared. Changes made on one device reach the other at the next sync: when the app opens, or with “Sync now” in Settings.

Sync works only within one Google account. On your phone, computer and any other device, sign in to the same account. With a different account the app creates a separate spreadsheet — and records from different devices never meet.

On a computer

Tolimoto Log also works on a computer — right in the browser. Handy for sorting receipts, entering old records or looking at statistics on a big screen.

  1. Open app.tolimoto.com in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. On the first screen choose “Continue in the browser”.
  2. Connect Google — the same account as on your phone. The app finds your spreadsheet, and all records from the phone appear on the computer.
  3. Installing is optional. In Chrome and Edge: the install icon on the right of the address bar. In Safari on a Mac: File → Add to Dock. The app then opens in its own window.
  • Menu on the left: “Add record”, the Garage, Log, Schedule and Stats tabs, and Settings at the bottom.
  • Forms open as a window in the middle of the screen. Close with Esc or the cross.
  • Several vehicles: switch with the dots under the card in the Garage.
  • Without Google, records on the computer are separate from the phone and stay only in that browser.

How not to lose your data

Without Google, your records live only in the app on this device. If the phone is lost or broken, or the app is deleted, the records are gone. Choose one of the two ways and set it up today.

1 · recommended Google spreadsheet

Everything is copied to your Google Drive automatically. New phone: install the app, sign in with the same account — everything comes back.

2 · without Google Backup file

Settings → “Keeping your data safe” → “Save a backup”. Save the JSON file off the phone: iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email it to yourself. The app reminds you once a month and shows the date of the last backup.

New phone

  1. Install the app (see above) and open it from its icon.
  2. On the “Where should your records live?” screen choose “Connect Google” — or “Restore from a backup” and pick the JSON file.

What deletes the data

  • Removing the app from the iPhone Home Screen deletes its records too. Make a backup first.
  • Clearing website data in the browser settings may wipe them as well.
  • Records entered in a Safari tab and in the installed app are separate. Install first, then log.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing, and it will stay that way: no ads, no subscriptions, no paid features. If you would like to support the project, buy us a litre of fuel.

Does Tolimoto see my records?

No. There is no Tolimoto server: data stays on your phone and, if you connect it, in your own Google Drive.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline; Google sync catches up when the network is back.

Can I keep several motorcycles and cars?

Yes, as many as you like. Each has its own schedule, log and statistics.

Can I use it on a computer?

Yes. Open app.tolimoto.com in a browser on your computer and connect Google — the same account as on your phone. See “On a computer” for details.

My phone records do not show on the computer

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Google account (it is shown in Settings). If it is the same account, tap “Sync now” on the phone, then on the computer.

Android?

The app works in Chrome on Android and installs from its menu. It was built and tested on iPhone first — if something looks off, write to us.

How do I show a buyer the service history?

Garage → tap the vehicle name → “Export history (PDF)”. More in “Selling: the history for the buyer”.
