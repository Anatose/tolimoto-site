In short: Tolimoto Log has no server and no user accounts of its own. What you record stays on your device. If you choose to connect Google, the app writes to a spreadsheet and a folder in your Google Drive and can see only the files it created. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Who we are

Tolimoto Log (the “app”, at app.tolimoto.com) and this website are published by Tolimoto. Questions about this policy: see Contact below.

What the app stores and where

The app keeps the records you enter — vehicles, odometer readings, fill-ups, service work, expenses, maintenance schedule and notes — in your browser's storage on your device (IndexedDB). This data is not sent to Tolimoto; we do not operate any server that receives it. You can export it at any time as JSON or CSV from Settings.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party SDKs.

Optional Google connection

Connecting Google is optional; the app works fully without it. If you sign in with Google, the app requests only these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — to create, read and update the files the app itself creates in your Google Drive: a folder named “Tolimoto Log”, a spreadsheet “Tolimoto Log” and a “Receipts” folder for photos you attach. This permission does not give access to any other files in your Drive.
  • email — to show which Google account is connected.

With this access the app copies your records into that spreadsheet and reads back changes you make there (two-way sync), and uploads photos of receipts and parts that you choose to attach. All of this happens directly between your device and Google's servers. Tolimoto never receives, stores or sees your Google data or your sign-in.

The access token issued by Google is kept in your browser's storage, expires after about one hour, and is renewed by Google sign-in when you use the app. You can disconnect in the app's Settings at any time and revoke access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already created in your Drive remain yours; you can keep or delete them.

Limited Use. Tolimoto Log's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. There is no one to share it with: it never reaches us.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: delete records in the app, or clear the site data for app.tolimoto.com in your browser settings (removing the home-screen app on iPhone also removes its data).
  • In Google: delete the “Tolimoto Log” folder in your Drive and revoke access as described above.
